Attestly Telegram Bot

Attestly turns your AI agents' operational traces — tool calls, model calls, human interventions, errors — into audit-ready EU AI Act Annex IV technical documentation, continuously, instead of as a quarterly scramble.

This repository is the open-source Telegram companion bot for Attestly. In Telegram it can:

  • Run a free 4-question EU AI Act risk classifier (/riskcheck)
  • Draft an Annex IV section straight from an uploaded trace file (/generate)
  • Answer common questions about the EU AI Act and Attestly automatically in groups
  • Moderate groups: welcome new members, filter spam/off-topic links, and support admin /ban and /promote
